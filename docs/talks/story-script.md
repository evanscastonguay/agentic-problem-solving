# AI-Assisted Development Presentation Script (Simple Map Story)

## One-Sentence Gift
AI-assisted development helps teams move from chat-level help to outcome-level
delegation by navigating a simple map: pick a goal, clear unknowns, choose a
route, and reach the result with agent teammates.

## Example Quest (Anchor for the Story)
Ship a customer-facing feature before a Friday demo.

## Slide 1: I Was Lost on the Map
- Visual: a blank map with no markers.
- On-screen text: "I was lost."
- Speaker notes:
  - "When I first played strategy games like SimCity or World of Warcraft, I
    wandered. I had tools, but no direction."
  - "This is how many teams use AI today: chat here, autocomplete there, but no
    shared objective."
  - "So I want to give you a simple map."
- Skill tie-in (spoken, not on slide): none yet, set the story.

## Slide 2: Pick the Destination
- Visual: add a single destination marker.
- On-screen text: "Define the win."
- Speaker notes:
  - "In games, you pick a quest. In work, we define the outcome."
  - "For our example: ship a customer feature by Friday."
  - "This is s3-define: objective, constraints, success criteria."
- Skill tie-in: s3-define.

## Slide 3: Clear the Fog
- Visual: fog-of-war with a small revealed area.
- On-screen text: "Explore first."
- Speaker notes:
  - "Before moving, you reveal the terrain. You read, scout, and learn."
  - "In software, that is exploring the codebase and clarifying the request."
  - "This is s1-explore and s2-clarify. It keeps us from walking into the
    wrong dungeon."
- Skill tie-in: s1-explore, s2-clarify.

## Slide 4: Choose the Route
- Visual: three routes labeled Fast, Safe, Risky.
- On-screen text: "Compare paths."
- Speaker notes:
  - "There is never just one path. We weigh speed, safety, and risk."
  - "This is s4-solutions, and compare makes the tradeoffs explicit."
  - "For our quest: patch now, refactor later, or stage the rollout."
- Skill tie-in: s4-solutions, compare.

## Slide 5: Bring the Team and Tools
- Visual: agent icons near the path; a simple tool belt.
- On-screen text: "Delegate outcomes."
- Speaker notes:
  - "The big shift: AI is not a chat box. It is a teammate."
  - "We give goals and context, not just single instructions."
  - "Start by naming which tools, systems, and access the agent actually has."
- Skill tie-in: s1-explore (the environment is part of the system).

## Slide 6: Plan Checkpoints and Save Points
- Visual: checkpoints on the route and a save flag.
- On-screen text: "Plan and validate."
- Speaker notes:
  - "We plan the run in phases and set checkpoints."
  - "s5-plan defines the route and risks. s6-implement is the expedition:
    a short sprint for a small change, full validation when the risk is high."
  - "The plan file is our save point: any session can resume from it."
- Skill tie-in: s5-plan, s6-implement, tdd, handoff.

## Slide 7: Re-Orient and Adjust
- Visual: "You are here" marker and a small logbook icon.
- On-screen text: "Note, locate, next."
- Speaker notes:
  - "Even good runs get messy. We pause, re-orient, and adapt."
  - "where tells us our position. doc keeps the travel log."
  - "next picks the next waypoint without losing the objective."
- Skill tie-in: where, doc, next.

## Slide 8: Reach the Outcome
- Visual: destination reached, map complete.
- On-screen text: "The gift."
- Speaker notes:
  - "The result is not just code. It is a reliable outcome delivered faster."
  - "We stay honest about what we skip and we verify the work."
  - "That is the gift of AI-assisted development: outcomes, not just outputs."
- Skill tie-in: close with the full loop, remind the map.

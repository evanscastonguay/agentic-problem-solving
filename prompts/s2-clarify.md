# 2 Clarify

> Step 2 of 6 — Ask targeted multiple-choice questions to clarify the request and confirm understanding before work begins. Use when a request is ambiguous, underspecified, or could be read more than one way, or when the user asks to clarify requirements.

- ask targeted multiple-choice questions to surface ambiguity
- prefer multiple-choice so the model names the ambiguity and you just pick
- confirm understanding and scope before proceeding
- focus on the areas most likely to be misunderstood
